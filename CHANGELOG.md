# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Initial public release

- Local-first teacher lesson workspace.
- Context form for topic, subject, class, learner age, duration, language and prior knowledge.
- Source notes and reference-file tracking.
- Structured AI lesson brief for manual handoff to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
- Guided teaching view with step navigation.
- Browser-local lesson library.
- Markdown, Word-compatible and print-to-PDF exports.
- Portable TeacherFlow teaching skill.
